# Reality-Calibrated Scenario Design

## Evidence boundary

The project separates three layers:

1. **Public fact** — disclosed U.S. Bancorp quarterly measures.
2. **Analytical inference** — a reasonable business interpretation of those measures.
3. **Synthetic assumption** — invented monthly product-level values used only for demonstration.

U.S. Bancorp does not publish complete monthly P&Ls for the three modeled product businesses. No synthetic figure should be described as an internal U.S. Bank number.

## Commercial Banking

Public calibration: Q2 2026 average commercial loans increased 5.0% sequentially and 14.1% year over year. The synthetic scenario therefore shows steady loan balance and interest-income growth, with moderate treasury and deposit fee momentum.

## Commercial Real Estate

Public calibration: Q2 2026 average CRE loans increased 3.7% sequentially and 5.8% year over year. CRE nonperforming loans increased from $522 million to $568 million sequentially, while broader delinquency ratios remained better than the prior year. The synthetic scenario combines recovering originations with localized provision pressure; it does not claim broad credit deterioration.

## Capital Markets

Public calibration: Q2 2026 capital markets revenue increased 35.8% sequentially and 62.5% year over year, driven by one month of BTIG contribution, client derivatives, corporate bond underwriting, favorable markets, and syndication activity. The synthetic June scenario shows a revenue step-up and integration-related expense pressure.
